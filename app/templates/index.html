Rules that hold under load

A link with a 100-click limit opens exactly 100 times, even when a crowd taps it at once. Expired links stop redirecting but keep their history.

Opens the right app

YouTube, Spotify and Instagram links open in the app on phones instead of a browser that asks visitors to sign in.

QR codes worth printing

Pick colours, rounded or dotted styles and your logo. Download PNG or SVG, or print a ready-made poster.

Analytics without the noise

Link previews, scripts and crawlers are counted separately, so your numbers show people. On your own site you can also measure time on page and scroll depth.

Safety before and after publishing

Suspicious and blocklisted URLs are flagged while you create the link. Admins can disable a link and every redirect stops at once.

Paste and done
